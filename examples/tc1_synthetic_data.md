# Research Report

**Query:** What are the real-world risks and benefits of using synthetic data to train or fine-tune large language models? Focus on data quality, bias, and evaluation.

## What are the primary advantages of using synthetic data for training or fine-tuning large language models (LLMs) in terms of data quality, scalability, and cost?
- Synthetic data can help overcome limitations in the supply of high-quality natural data for training large language models (LLMs).  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2410.12896)_
- Synthetic data techniques enable scalability in LLM training by generating diverse data types, such as rephrased text and generated textbooks, which can supplement or replace natural web data.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://aclanthology.org/2025.emnlp-main.544/, https://arxiv.org/html/2410.12896)_
- Pre-training LLMs on a mixture of natural web data and synthetic rephrases improves i.i.d. validation loss compared to using web data alone.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2603.18534)_
- Synthetic data augmentation is particularly valuable when pre-training is constrained by data availability rather than computational resources.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2603.18534)_
- Synthetic data can reduce the cost of acquiring high-quality, task-specific datasets for fine-tuning LLMs, which is often a bottleneck in model improvement.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: 35652078141)_
- Synthetic data generation strategies must balance cost and effectiveness to avoid inefficiencies in LLM training pipelines.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: 35652078141)_
- Synthetic data can help mitigate the depletion of high-quality web data, which is a growing concern for LLM pre-training.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.19551)_
- Scaling laws of synthetic data demonstrate that its use can improve LLM performance, especially as computational resources approach infinity.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.19551)_
- Synthetic data enables fine-tuning of smaller models in low-resource settings, reducing the need for large-scale computational deployment.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2310.01119)_
- Real labeled data for LLM fine-tuning is often expensive, scarce, or legally restricted, making synthetic data a practical alternative.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: https://blog.premai.io/how-to-generate-synthetic-training-data-for-llm-fine-tuning-2026-guide/)_
- Synthetic data can fill gaps in training datasets, but improper generation strategies may lead to model collapse or degraded performance.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://blog.premai.io/how-to-generate-synthetic-training-data-for-llm-fine-tuning-2026-guide/)_
- Quality filtering of synthetic data is critical to prevent failure modes and ensure effective LLM fine-tuning.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://blog.premai.io/how-to-generate-synthetic-training-data-for-llm-fine-tuning-2026-guide/)_
- Synthetic data generation strategies in production require careful tool selection and adherence to best practices to avoid pitfalls.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://blog.premai.io/how-to-generate-synthetic-training-data-for-llm-fine-tuning-2026-guide/)_

## How does synthetic data generation impact the bias and fairness of LLMs compared to real-world datasets?
- Synthetic data generated by LLMs can inherit and even amplify biases present in the original training data, potentially exacerbating fairness issues in downstream tasks.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04419v2)_
- LLM-based synthetic data generation may propagate or introduce biases related to sensitive attributes (e.g., gender, race) in high-stakes domains like healthcare, impacting fairness.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.19082)_
- Synthetic data generation methods often struggle to simultaneously achieve fairness and privacy, as fairness algorithms may not fully mitigate biases introduced by generative models.  _(insufficient, conf=0.80; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2501.01785v1)_
- LLMs used for synthetic data generation can reflect societal biases embedded in their training corpora, leading to unfair representations in the generated data.  _(supported, conf=0.80; sources: https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2305.15041, https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10755v1)_
- Fairness-aware synthetic data generation techniques, such as learning and distilling fair representations, can reduce bias in generated datasets compared to real-world data.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2408.10755v1)_
- Causally fair synthetic data generation methods aim to mitigate bias by ensuring sensitive attributes do not unfairly influence outcomes, improving fairness over real-world datasets.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.19082)_
- Synthetic data generated by LLMs may lack the diversity of real-world datasets, potentially introducing new biases or failing to represent underrepresented groups adequately.  _(insufficient, conf=0.80; sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2310.07849)_
- The fluency of LLM-generated synthetic text can mask underlying biases, making it difficult to detect fairness issues without comprehensive evaluation frameworks.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04419v2)_
- Guiding synthetic data generation with non-differentiable fairness objectives can help align LLM outputs with fairness goals, reducing bias compared to unconstrained generation.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2407.01490)_

## What are the key risks associated with synthetic data, such as hallucinations, overfitting, or distribution shifts, and how do they affect model performance?
- Synthetic data can introduce hallucinations, where the generated data contains fabricated or unrealistic patterns that do not reflect real-world distributions, degrading model performance.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12397v1, https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/blog/synthetic-data-real-harm/)_
- Overfitting is a key risk of synthetic data, as models trained on synthetic datasets may memorize artificial patterns rather than generalize to real-world data, leading to poor performance on unseen examples.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2305.09235v2, https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12397v1)_
- Distribution shifts occur when synthetic data fails to accurately replicate the statistical properties of real-world data, causing models to perform poorly when deployed in real-world scenarios.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12397v1, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kazdan25a.html)_
- Model collapse is a risk when synthetic data is recursively generated from prior models, leading to progressive degradation of data quality and model performance over time.  _(supported, conf=0.90; sources: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kazdan25a.html, https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/blog/synthetic-data-real-harm/)_
- Synthetic data may amplify biases present in the original training data or introduce new biases, particularly if the generative model itself is biased, exacerbating fairness issues in downstream tasks.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2305.09235v2, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12778113/)_
- Errors in synthetic data generation can propagate to downstream tasks, leading to incorrect or unreliable model predictions, especially if the synthetic data is not rigorously validated.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2305.09235v2, https://ojs.bonviewpress.com/index.php/AIA/article/download/6620/1769)_
- Synthetic data may lack the diversity or edge cases present in real-world data, limiting the model's ability to handle rare or complex scenarios effectively.  _(insufficient, conf=0.80; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2406.12397v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14023v2)_
- The use of synthetic data can create a feedback loop where AI-generated content is re-ingested into training datasets, leading to 'Habsburg AI'—a degradation of quality and increasing abstraction over time.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/kazdan25a.html, https://www.adalovelaceinstitute.org/blog/synthetic-data-real-harm/)_
- Synthetic data may not be suitable for all applications, particularly in sensitive domains like healthcare, where inaccuracies can lead to significant real-world harm.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://ojs.bonviewpress.com/index.php/AIA/article/download/6620/1769, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12778113/)_
- While synthetic data can improve model resilience and mitigate data shortages, its use introduces a trade-off between performance gains and the risk of hallucinations or unrealistic outputs.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2503.14023v2, https://ojs.bonviewpress.com/index.php/AIA/article/download/6620/1769)_

## What evaluation metrics or frameworks are used to assess the quality and reliability of synthetic data for LLM training?
- Evaluation metrics for synthetic data in LLM training often focus on quality, diversity, and complexity as key dimensions.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.02980)_
- The LLM Data Auditor framework provides a metric-oriented survey to assess the quality and trustworthiness of synthetic data, emphasizing systematic evaluation approaches.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17717v2)_
- A multi-faceted evaluation framework for synthetic data generated by LLMs includes metrics such as fidelity, utility, and privacy to ensure robustness.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.14445)_
- Evaluating synthetic data involves assessing its utility for downstream tasks, such as fine-tuning or post-training language models, to determine its effectiveness.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2412.03679v2)_
- SynQP is a framework that introduces metrics for evaluating both the quality and privacy risk of synthetic data, particularly in health applications.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: 42692877002)_
- SynAE is a framework designed to measure the quality of synthetic data specifically for tool-calling agent evaluations, ensuring alignment with real-world execution traces.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2605.22564)_
- RoSE (Round-robin Synthetic Data Evaluation) is a method for selecting LLM generators without human test sets by comparing synthetic data outputs in a round-robin fashion.  _(supported, conf=1.00; sources: https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-long.258.pdf)_
- SynthTextEval is a toolkit for generating and evaluating synthetic text data, particularly for high-stakes domains, with a focus on fluency and domain-specific relevance.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.07229)_
- Common evaluation metrics for synthetic data include fidelity (how closely it resembles real data), utility (its effectiveness in training models), and diversity (coverage of edge cases).  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17717v2, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.14445)_
- Privacy-preserving metrics, such as those in SynQP, are critical for synthetic data used in sensitive domains like healthcare to prevent re-identification risks.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: 42692877002)_

## How do industry practices (e.g., data augmentation, adversarial filtering) mitigate or exacerbate risks in synthetic data usage?
- Industry practices like data augmentation can introduce errors in downstream ML tasks if synthetic data does not accurately replicate the statistical properties of real data.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2305.09235v2)_
- Synthetic data generation may exacerbate risks by inheriting or amplifying biases from the original data, particularly in LLM-based augmentation, which can degrade performance on downstream tasks.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2502.04419v2)_
- Adversarial filtering and other generative model techniques can mitigate risks by improving the utility of synthetic data, ensuring it closely mirrors the original data's characteristics.  _(insufficient, conf=0.80; sources: https://royalsociety.org/-/media/policy/projects/privacy-enhancing-technologies/Synthetic_Data_Survey-24.pdf)_
- Synthetic data can reduce privacy risks by replacing sensitive real data, but its effectiveness depends on the generative model's ability to preserve utility while obscuring identifiable information.  _(supported, conf=0.90; sources: https://www.edps.europa.eu/press-publications/publications/techsonar/synthetic-data)_
- Synthetic data may exacerbate risks if the generative process fails to capture rare or edge cases present in real data, leading to gaps in downstream task performance.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.03791)_
- Best practices for synthetic data, such as rigorous validation and bias monitoring, can mitigate risks by ensuring high-quality, representative synthetic datasets for AI initiatives.  _(supported, conf=0.90; sources: https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/streamline-accelerate-ai-initiatives-synthetic-data)_
- Synthetic image generation can either mitigate or exacerbate risks depending on its utility and privacy trade-offs, with some methods failing to fully replace real data in sensitive applications.  _(supported, conf=0.99; sources: https://www.usenix.org/system/files/usenixsecurity25-chung-yunsung.pdf)_
- Bias in synthetic data can be mitigated through techniques like fairness-aware generative models, but improper implementation may instead reinforce or introduce new biases.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://datalere.com/articles/mitigating-the-risk-of-bias-in-synthetic-data-for-ai)_

## Are there documented cases where synthetic data has led to measurable improvements or failures in real-world LLM applications?
- Scaling laws for synthetic data in LLM pre-training reveal measurable benefits, such as improved model performance when synthetic data is mixed with real data at optimal ratios, but also highlight pitfalls like performance degradation when synthetic data dominates the training mix.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://aclanthology.org/anthology-files/pdf/emnlp/2025.emnlp-main.544.pdf)_
- Empirical studies across model scales and synthetic data mixing ratios demonstrate that synthetic data can enhance reasoning capabilities and instruction-following performance, but excessive reliance on synthetic data may lead to model collapse or reduced generalization.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2510.05133)_
- Synthetic data generated by LLMs for text classification tasks has shown potential in reducing the cost and time investment of curating real-world datasets, but its effectiveness varies depending on the complexity of the task and the quality of the synthetic examples.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2310.07849v1, https://mingyin.org/paper/EMNLP-23/emnlp2023.pdf)_
- Synthetic data can negatively impact the quality of text embedders, as models trained on synthetic corpora may produce embeddings that are less representative of real-world data distributions, leading to degraded performance in downstream tasks.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://aclanthology.org/2025.findings-acl.1160.pdf)_
- NVIDIA's Nemotron 3 Ultra model leverages synthetic data to improve reasoning efficiency and throughput in long-running agent workflows, demonstrating measurable gains in real-world applications such as complex task orchestration.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: 49250754091)_
- Synthetic datasets generated by LLMs may not reliably represent real-world data, leading to failures in benchmarking and evaluation when synthetic data is used as a proxy for real-world performance without proper validation.  _(supported, conf=0.95; sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2409.11968v1)_
